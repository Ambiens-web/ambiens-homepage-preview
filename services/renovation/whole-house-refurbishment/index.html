How a project runs

Five stages. Planning permission is only needed for some projects, and we tell you at the first visit whether yours does.

  1. 01

    Visit and survey

    We meet at your house, measure, and agree what you want to achieve.

    Ambiens
  2. 02

    Design and 3D

    Layout, joinery and finishes drawn in 3D. For structural work an architect and engineer join here.

    Ambiens with partners
  3. 03

    Planning permission

    If your project needs it, the architect prepares and submits the application and we manage it through to a decision. Exempt work gets a written exemption check instead.

    Architect, managed by Ambiens
  4. 04

    Build and make

    Registered partners carry out the building work while your joinery is made to measure.

    Partners, coordinated by Ambiens
  5. 05

    Fit and hand over

    We fit the joinery, check every detail with you and hand over the finished home.

    Ambiens

    Whole-house refurbishment

    Bringing an existing house up to date throughout, without changing its structure.

    What is included

    • Rewiring and new consumer unit
    • Replumbing and new heating
    • Plastering and decorating
    • New floors throughout
    • New bathrooms
    • Joinery throughout: kitchen, wardrobes, doors

    Why it matters

    Doing all rooms in one programme is faster and cheaper than room by room, and hides new wiring and pipes before the finishes go on.

    What you get

    A house that is updated from wiring to finishes, ready to move into.

    Who does it

    Builder, electrician, plumber, managed by Ambiens

    Your stages and indicative timings

    Each stage has an indicative duration. Some run in parallel, including joinery production and building work. Timings are confirmed after the survey.

    • Survey and first visitApprox. 1–2 weeks
    • Design: layout, interiors and 3DApprox. 1–3 weeks
    • Specification and builder selectionApprox. 3–6 weeks
    • Whole-house refurbishmentApprox. 8–14 weeks
    • BOLONI joinery made to measureApprox. 6–8 weeks
    • Joinery installationApprox. 1–3 weeks
    • Snagging and handoverApprox. 1–2 weeks
