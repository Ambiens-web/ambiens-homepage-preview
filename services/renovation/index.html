How a project runs

Five stages. Planning permission is only needed for some projects, and we tell you at the first visit whether yours does.

  1. 01

    Visit and survey

    We meet at your house, measure, and agree what you want to achieve.

    Ambiens
  2. 02

    Design and 3D

    Layout, joinery and finishes drawn in 3D. For structural work an architect and engineer join here.

    Ambiens with partners
  3. 03

    Planning permission

    If your project needs it, the architect prepares and submits the application and we manage it through to a decision. Exempt work gets a written exemption check instead.

    Architect, managed by Ambiens
  4. 04

    Build and make

    Registered partners carry out the building work while your joinery is made to measure.

    Partners, coordinated by Ambiens
  5. 05

    Fit and hand over

    We fit the joinery, check every detail with you and hand over the finished home.

    Ambiens

    Design

    Every project starts with a design for the whole house, not just one room. You see it in 3D before anything is built or made.

    1. Survey and brief

      We measure the house, look at how you live in it, and agree what the project needs to achieve and what it should cost.

      Ambiens

    2. Layout and 3D

      New layouts drawn and shown in 3D, so you can walk through the rooms and change them before work starts.

      Ambiens

    3. Interior design

      Materials, colours, lighting, flooring, bathrooms and the joinery, chosen together so the whole house reads as one.

      Ambiens

    4. Architecture and planning

      For extensions, structural changes and new homes, a registered architect draws the scheme, prepares the planning application and the building regulation drawings.

      Architect and engineer, managed by Ambiens

    5. Specification

      A full written specification, so builders price the same thing and you know exactly what you are getting.

      Ambiens
